3. Project Origin & Purpose

Rajasthan Civic Connect was designed as a modern civic technology (GovTech) solution to address municipal service delivery challenges across Rajasthan. Traditional complaint mechanisms often lack transparency, precise location tracking, real-time worker accountability, and clear SLA monitoring.

By digitizing the complaint lifecycle with Leaflet map geo-tagging, multi-role access control, and automated ward routing, the platform bridges the gap between citizens, ward nodal officers, field force workers, and municipal commissioners.

10. User Roles & Capability Matrix

RolePortal AccessKey CapabilitiesAccess Restrictions
Citizen Citizen Workspace Create complaints, save local drafts, select map locations, track timeline, rate work, request reopen. Cannot inspect other citizens' complaints or modify officer assignments.
Officer Department Console Review department complaints, dispatch field force, monitor SLA breaches, export CSV/PDF reports. Restricted to assigned municipal department records.
Worker Field Executive Desk View assigned task queue, update work status, upload on-site before/after completion proof photos. Cannot change department allocations or reassign tasks.
Admin Super Admin Portal System-wide user directory, department settings, officer roster, emergency escalations, platform analytics. Full administrative access across all municipal nodes.
